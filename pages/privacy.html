About your information

A meeting, with privacy in mind.

Here is how this scheduler uses information to arrange and manage your meeting.

What your booking includes

Your name, email address, optional topic, chosen meeting type, location, time, time zone, and language are used to create and manage your booking. Booking records are kept in the private scheduler; there is currently no automatic deletion schedule.

The services involved

Google Calendar receives event and guest details to create your meeting and send its calendar invitation. Google Meet or Zoom supplies the video meeting link when that service is selected. Resend delivers booking confirmations and reminders. Cloudflare hosts the scheduler and provides security, including Turnstile spam protection.

Availability stays private

Events from the organizer’s selected Google and iCloud calendars are read to check for scheduling conflicts. Existing calendar details are not shown to people booking a meeting; only available times are displayed.

Your private booking link

Your management link lets you view your booking and make changes within the allowed deadline. Keep it private: someone with that link can manage the booking. Guest details and management links are not saved in browser storage. Your appearance preference is saved in this browser.

No marketing signup

Booking a meeting does not subscribe you to a mailing list. The scheduler uses your email for meeting invitations, booking messages, and reminders.
