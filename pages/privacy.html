About your information

A meeting, with privacy in mind.

When you book a meeting, Scheduler uses the information you share to arrange it and handle any changes.

What your booking includes

To arrange and manage your meeting, Scheduler uses your name, email address and any topic you choose to share, along with the meeting type, location, time, time zone and language. Booking records stay in the private scheduler and aren't automatically deleted.

The services involved

Google Calendar gets the meeting and guest details so it can create the event and send your invitation. For video meetings, Google Meet or Zoom provides the link, depending on the service selected. Resend sends confirmations and reminders. Cloudflare hosts Scheduler and helps keep it secure, including Turnstile to protect against spam.

Availability stays private

Scheduler checks the organizer’s selected Google and iCloud calendars for conflicts. You’ll see the times you can book, but not the details of events already on those calendars.

Your private booking link

Your private booking link lets you view your meeting and make changes before the deadline. Keep it to yourself: anyone with the link can manage your booking. Scheduler doesn't save guest details or management links in browser storage. It does save your appearance preference in this browser.

No marketing signup

Booking a meeting won’t add you to a mailing list. Your email is used for the invitation, messages about your booking and reminders.
